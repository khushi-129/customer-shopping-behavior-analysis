# Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis
End-to-end data analytics project: Kaggle dataset → Python data cleaning → MySQL analysis → Power BI dashboard for customer behavior insights.

## Overview

This project analyzes customer shopping behavior using Python, MySQL, and Power BI. The objective was to transform raw customer transaction data into meaningful business insights by identifying purchasing patterns, customer segments, revenue trends, and subscription behavior.

The project follows an end-to-end analytics workflow, from data cleaning and transformation to SQL-based analysis and dashboard visualization.

---

## Dataset

- Source: Kaggle
- Dataset: Customer Shopping Behavior Dataset
- Records: ~3,900 customer transactions
- Features include:
  - Customer Demographics
  - Product Categories
  - Purchase Amount
  - Review Ratings
  - Subscription Status
  - Shipping Type
  - Previous Purchases
  - Purchase Frequency

## Steps Performed

### 1. Data Cleaning & Preprocessing (Python)
- Loaded and explored the dataset.
- Handled missing values.
- Standardized column names.
- Created age group segments.
- Converted purchase frequency into a structured format.
- Removed redundant columns.
- Prepared the cleaned dataset for analysis.

### 2. Data Analysis (MySQL)
Performed SQL queries to analyze:
- Revenue by category
- Revenue by age group
- Customer segmentation
- Subscription vs non-subscription behavior
- Product performance
- Customer spending patterns

### 3. Dashboard Development (Power BI)
Created an interactive dashboard to visualize:
- Total Customers
- Average Purchase Amount
- Average Review Rating
- Revenue by Category
- Sales by Category
- Revenue by Age Group
- Sales by Age Group
- Subscription Distribution

## Results

### Key Insights
- Clothing generated the highest revenue and sales volume.
- Young Adult customers contributed the largest share of revenue.
- Most customers were non-subscribers, highlighting opportunities for membership growth.
- Customer purchasing behavior varied across age groups and product categories.
- Subscription programs showed potential for increasing customer retention and recurring revenue.

### Business Recommendations
- Increase subscription adoption through loyalty programs and exclusive benefits.
- Focus marketing campaigns on high-value customer segments.
- Expand inventory for top-performing product categories.
- Implement retention strategies for repeat customers.
- Use targeted promotions to improve customer engagement and sales.
